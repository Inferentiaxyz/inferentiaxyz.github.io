Our method

Every project starts with a direct conversation. Then we choose the right path together: start right away or validate the idea first.

01

Client meeting

We listen to your goals and needs with targeted questions to fully understand the context.

Path A

Clear requirements

Goals, data and requirements are already defined: we start right away.

Path B 02

03

04
